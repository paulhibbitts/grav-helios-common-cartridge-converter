# Module 5 - Reflections of online learning and implications for practice.

The course outcome is to:  ***Reflect on your experiences as an online learner and show how it will influence you online teaching.***

The purpose of this module is to reflect on what you have learned in this course and to share these reflections with others.

[objectives]
1. Reflect on the ITO course and share the following:
   - What did you learn?
   - Why is it important to you?
   - How you will use it? (have the barriers you identified in week one been removed?
